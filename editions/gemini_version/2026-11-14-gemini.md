## 1. Technological Paradigm Shifts: Entropy & Non-Consensus Anomalies

### Chain-of-Thought Monitorability Erosion & Latent Reasoning:
According to [OpenAI Alignment Research's November 10, 2026 study](https://alignment.openai.com/cot-monitorability-model-lifecycle), GPT-6 Astra reached a 30.9-minute median human-task time horizon on math benchmarks _with reasoning turned off_ (versus 7 minutes projected for late 2028), driven smoothly by falling base-model perplexity. ### Two Kinds of Test-Time Scaling: Compute Extrapolation via Compaction:
[Forethought's November 9, 2026 research](https://www.forethought.org/research/two-kinds-of-test-time-scaling) distinguishes standard test-time scaling (capped at training-time per-task budgets) from **compute extrapolation** via chunked compaction loops (such as Delethink, Opus 4.6, and GPT-5.1-Codex-Max), which extend gains 10x to 1,000x+ past training limits. Concurrently, RLVR proved the [Aaronson-Ambainis Conjecture](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=10295) on November 8, 2026. Compute extrapolation shifts R&D races toward massive inference-time moonshots while making dangerous-capability audits prohibitively expensive.

### Stateless Enterprise MCP & The Four Missing Agent Layers:
[Jitterbit's November 5, 2026 analysis](https://www.jitterbit.com/nl/blog/new-enterprise-capability-layer-for-ai-part-2/) notes that the 2026 MCP specification has shifted to a stateless HTTP core with cacheable responses, OAuth, and a Tasks extension for durable asynchronous handles.
