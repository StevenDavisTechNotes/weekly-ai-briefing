Strictly do NOT include markdown tables, grid matrices, or comparative tables (as they do not translate well to audio playback). Strictly omit corporate deal noise, commoditized press releases (e.g. standard vendor partnerships, generic fundraising rounds), and consensus rehash. Focus intensely on "entropy"—what is unexpected, surprising, counter-intuitive, or anomalous in current technical developments.

Strategic Mental Model Shifts: Articulate fundamental revisions to our overarching structural paradigm (e.g., how the multi-tier intelligence model is mutating into a contextual composition model, routing layers, specialized domain cloud models, enterprise revenue distribution, and labor models).
