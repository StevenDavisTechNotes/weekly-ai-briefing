research was done, and every figure is stated as the editions reported it.

## Table rows: why chosen and correlations

- Nvidia accelerator position: suggested by the reader and central to M-09's collateral (GPU-backed loans). The
  editions give no share figure, so the reading is a judgment; see the checks below. Most correlated with AI and
  data-center credit access and with Memory and equipment capacity.
- Treasury key rates: suggested by the reader as key rate exposure at 2, 10 and 30 years; the table tracks the market
  rates, since the firm's own exposure is not in the editions. Most correlated with Goods and producer inflation.
- Goods and producer inflation: the channel by which the build-out raises its own cost of capital (M-10). Most
  correlated with Treasury key rates and Memory and equipment capacity.
- AI and data-center credit access: the first place M-09 predicts stress will show, and where the firm acts. Most
  correlated with Frontier lab commitments and Anti-data-center activism (through later-year political risk).
- Hyperscaler free cash flow: tests whether sponsors fund the build-out themselves. Most correlated with Frontier lab
  commitments; kept separate because hyperscalers and labs can diverge.
- Frontier lab commitments: measures the concentration at the center of the closed loop. Most correlated with AI and
  data-center credit access.
- Anti-data-center activism: suggested by the reader; the editions support it strongly from 2026-10-24. Most
  correlated with Grid capacity price, since capacity prices drove the votes.
- Grid capacity price: a market-priced measure of the power constraint, independent of polls. Most correlated with
  Anti-data-center activism; kept because it can move without any political event.
- Memory and equipment capacity: uses the reader's example scale; supplies the goods-inflation channel and sets
  hardware costs. Most correlated with Goods and producer inflation.
- Agent admission by counterparties: tests M-05 and decides what agentic services the firm can build. Most
  correlated with Frontier oversight coverage only weakly; it is among the most independent rows.
- Frontier oversight coverage: the observable for M-03 at the labs. Most correlated with Cost of AI-enabled offense,
  since both track capability outrunning controls.
- Cost of AI-enabled offense: the observable for M-04. Most correlated with Frontier oversight coverage.
- Enterprise AI spend concentration: the observable for M-06 and for the revenue base under M-09. Most correlated with
  Frontier lab commitments.
- Post-quantum deadlines: the observable for M-08 and the firm's migration exposure. Independent of the other rows.
- Robot deployment terms: the observable for M-07 and for when industrial-automation capex becomes financeable.
  Independent of most rows.

Rows considered and not used: a labor-share row for M-12 (left out to stay within 15 rows; M-12's falsifier names the
