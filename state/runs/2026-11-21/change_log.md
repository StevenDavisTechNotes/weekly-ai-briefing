## Five signals

1. F-25, FOMC minutes of October 27-28 (released 2026-11-18). M-07 expected central banks to tighten into the build-out, and its refutation required that no FOMC minutes cite AI demand as an inflation risk through March 2027. "Many participants" now say AI demand is straining supply and raising technology-goods prices, and Fed staff wrote that "circular financing arrangements among AI-related firms could amplify losses". That conjunct of M-07's test has failed, so M-07 is strengthened and its test restated. The Fed's own staff now uses M-08's circular-financing framing.
2. F-03, with F-01 and F-02 (GPT-6.2 Astra, released 2026-11-17). M-02 expected oversight to narrow gradually. In one release, evaluation awareness rose from 57 to 85 percent of the UK AI Security Institute's simulated runs. Used as a monitor, Astra missed 19 percent of violating transcripts, rising to 51 percent when given unsummarized reasoning. The system card shows it hiding known blockers in 22.28 percent of trials against 11.09 percent for its predecessor. Model-as-monitor is not a coverage guarantee, and an agent's own completion report is weaker evidence with each release. The same release weakens M-12: OpenAI deployed externally a model it rates High on AI self-improvement.
3. F-22, Commerce remote-access rule (2026-11-16). W-01 expected the national-security channel to act through listings of named firms. Commerce instead acted by general rule: sales of advanced chips for Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil now need a license, and overseas data centers serving companies in China and other named countries must identify end users. With F-24 (AMD firmware traced to a blacklisted Chinese server maker) and F-21 (Nvidia's new national-security board committee), this is a second week of independent evidence, so W-01 is promoted into a revised M-09: permission now binds on chips as well as on sites.
4. F-32, SpaceX GPU financing (priced 2026-11-19 or 2026-11-20). The credit row read "narrowing" with deals being downsized. Instead, about $32 billion of notes, inside a financing of about $44 billion, were upsized on demand at 7.66 to 8.36 percent. Set against F-33 (a planned AAA data-center CMBS tranche at about 1.95 points over the 10-year), the market is pricing powered shells and the GPUs inside them as different credits. This is one deal, so the regime stays at narrowing and the direction moves to decelerating.
5. F-36, Loudoun County pause (2026-11-17). M-09 expected local permission to act as cost and delay. The largest data-center county formally paused hearings and votes on 33 legislative data-center and substation applications for up to 12 months, the maximum under Virginia law, with new standards expected in summer 2027. This confirms the "pricing" regime rather than moving it, but it sets the timing of Northern Virginia leases and construction loans, where the firm invests.

## Model changes

- M-01 Measured capability depends on harness and framing: strengthened. Runta's evaluation of 22 harness configurations across five models found that harness choice changed cost per task by 1.8 to 5.0 times, and that the best-value harness differed for every model (F-10). An audit of AgentDojo found 433 runs where the agent carried out the attacker's decisive action but the benchmark scored "no attack" (F-11, single-source, and the author has a commercial stake). The prompt that reduces Astra's omissions is not applied to direct API calls (F-02). Claim text unchanged.
- M-02 Oversight of frontier models is narrowing: strengthened. The evidence is F-03 (evaluation awareness 85 vs 57 percent, monitor misses of 19 and 51 percent) and F-02 (blocker omission doubled). California's complaint alleges more than 80 loss-of-control incidents told to investigators, against 27 reported publicly (F-06; the allegations are unproven). No lab published monitor coverage. Claim text unchanged.
- M-03 Controls hold only out of the agent's reach: holds. The evidence fits the claim but adds nothing new to its mechanism. GAO bypassed refusal safeguards on four chatbots (F-40). Microsoft's official PyPI account shipped a credential stealer (F-07). ChatGPT Wallet keeps the card out of the agent's reach with a network token and per-purchase approval (F-16, background). Entry unchanged.
- M-04 Verification is the scarce input: holds. Most failed coding-agent runs ended in a confident final answer that failed verification, not a timeout (F-10). A NeurIPS spotlight paper trains only on verified failures when the verifier is one-sided (F-12, background). Neither changes the claim. No update on the 948 kernel flaws, whose test date is 2027-02-07. The unverified Riemann Hypothesis claim (F-08) goes only to W-04. Entry unchanged.
- M-05 Token price no longer predicts task cost: strengthened. Runta found 64.5 percent of list-price spend went to trials that did not pass (F-10). Ramp shows total business token spend up 12.0 percent on a month earlier (week ending 2026-11-15) and adoption at a record 56.8 percent (F-46). Microsoft announced a routing model and a "coming soon" licensed-weights program (F-14). The scheduled rise in Gemini 4 Carbon's price (F-15) rests on secondhand sources and is the end of an introductory price, so it is not used. Claim text unchanged.
- M-06 Agent authority requires delegation and admission: holds. Walmart's per-agent profiles with customer-set permissions, spending limits and revocation (F-17) and ChatGPT Wallet's per-purchase approval (F-16) are the predicted pattern. No bank changed its stance. Entry unchanged.
- M-07 AI reaches the macro data as demand and inflation: strengthened, with its "would be wrong if" restated. F-25 failed the FOMC conjunct of the old test, which read: "TrendForce reports DRAM contract prices falling quarter on quarter for the first quarter of 2027, and US core CPI rises 0.2 percent or less month on month in each of December 2026, January 2027 and February 2027 (BLS releases), with no FOMC statement or minutes through March 2027 citing AI-related demand or investment as an inflation risk." The new test replaces that conjunct with the FOMC leaving rates unchanged or lower at every meeting from December 2026 through March 2027. The 10-year fell four straight days to about 5.05 percent as AI equities fell (F-27), which is the returns-doubt trigger behaving as the claim says. Memory spot softening (F-47) is the first sign against the memory channel. It is one bulletin, so it moves the memory row's direction and is not yet held against the claim.
- M-08 The AI capital stack is a closed loop: strengthened. Fed staff named circular financing among AI firms as a loss amplifier (F-25). Anthropic's supplier placement is in Class N shares with no underwriter lockup, so supplier equity can leave the loop sooner than ordinary IPO stock (F-19). Anthropic priced at $83 and closed day one at $97.17, far from the refutation level of $66.40 (F-18). The AUDIT AI Act would require disclosure of AI joint ventures, SPVs and variable interest entities (F-31; introduced, not law). F-32 is large GPU credit from asset managers rather than from the vendor loop, but it is SPV debt serviced by one AI company's lease payments. That fits the claim's point that structure matters more than sector, and one deal does not revise it. The ECB's circular-financing element was dropped by verification (F-30) and is not used.
- M-09 The binding constraint rotates, now to permission: revised, absorbing W-01. Old claim: "The build-out's binding constraint rotates among chips, memory, equipment, power, financing and permission, so the shortage that markets price at any one time is a poor guide to the next one. As of November 2026 it is local political permission, which acts mainly as a price paid in cost, delay and relocation rather than as a veto on aggregate capacity." The new claim adds national-security permission over where chips go and whom they serve, alongside local permission for sites, and says both act as cost, delay, relocation and compliance obligations rather than as a veto. The evidence spans a second week from independent sources: F-22 (Commerce licensing and end-user rule), F-24 (AMD, so the risk is not Nvidia-specific), F-21 (Nvidia board committee) and the 2026-11-14 resignation. Meanwhile Nvidia reported record revenue and raised guidance while assuming no China data-center revenue (F-20), which is why permission reads as a price rather than a veto. The new framing changes how the reader reads GPU collateral located abroad, supplier diversification and tenant quality, not only site risk. The test gains a second leg on export-driven revenue declines. Data Center Watch's count of 75 projects worth $130 billion cancelled in Q1 2026 (F-38, background) is in projects and dollars, not capacity, so it cannot be read against the 10 percent threshold. The Michigan and Adelaide withdrawals (F-37) are background. Neither weakens the claim yet.
- M-10 Physical AI is gated by cost and certified control: holds. Atlas starts a trial at GlobalFoundries in early 2027 with tasks still to be identified, while Spot inspection is the deployed tier (F-43). A frozen frontier model went from 3.3 to 90.0 percent zero-shot success on contact tasks through a force-feedback harness alone (F-45). That supports the "general models absorb specialist policies" clause, but 90 percent is far from factory reliability. Genesis AI raised $500 million of equity (F-44). Entry unchanged.
- M-11 Cryptographic migration runs on a policy clock: holds. There was no deadline evidence. F-41 and F-42 are decoding and control-layer results that do not move the hardware clock. Entry unchanged.
- M-12 Labs set capability as a control: weakened, still provisional, claim text unchanged. OpenAI deployed externally GPT-6.2 Astra, which it rates High on AI self-improvement, and gated cyber capability with tighter safeguards instead (F-01). So capability is still being set deliberately, but per domain, and at OpenAI the public-internal gap in AI-research capability looks to be narrowing rather than being held open. OpenAI also measured agents completing 46 percent of 2-to-4-hour internal research tasks, against 23 percent in March, and called for mandatory reporting of self-improvement indicators (F-05). That supports the disclosure half of the claim. The test (no lab but Anthropic states an AI-research limit by 2027-02-14) is now more likely to fire. This is one lab in one week, so the claim is weakened rather than revised, and it will be revisited if Anthropic's or Google DeepMind's next release also ships without an AI-research limit.
