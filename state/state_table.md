## Row definitions

### US Treasury curve

- Measure: Daily closing yields on the 2-, 10- and 30-year US Treasury from the Treasury's daily par yield curve, read alongside the same-day move in the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index to classify each large move as AI-returns-driven (yields down, AI equities down) or inflation-driven (yields up, AI equities down).
- Regime scale: none.
- Why it is in the table: M-07, M-08.
- Last reading: Treasury daily par yield curve (read 2026-11-28): 10-year 5.12 percent on 2026-11-16, 5.04 on 2026-11-19, 4.92 on 2026-11-20, 4.85 on 2026-11-25 and 4.94 on 2026-11-27; 2-year 4.83, 4.76, 4.63, 4.61 and 4.69 on the same dates; 30-year 5.46, 5.40, 5.31, 5.24 and 5.33 (no data for 2026-11-26). On 2026-11-25 the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index was down 7.3 percent at 11:28 a.m. ET (Reuters via Kitco), the same day as the October PCE release, so that day's fall classifies as AI-returns-driven.

### Fed policy and inflation pulse

- Measure: The FOMC target range; the BLS month-on-month headline and core CPI; and futures-implied odds for the next meeting as reported by a named outlet.
- Regime scale: easing (cuts at consecutive meetings); on hold (no change expected at the next meeting); gradual tightening (25 bp increases expected, not at every meeting); accelerated tightening (50 bp or larger increases, or increases at consecutive meetings).
- Why it is in the table: M-07.
- Last reading: target range 3.75 to 4 percent, held on 2026-10-28 in a ten to two vote with two dissents for an increase, as reported in the 2026-10-31 edition citing the Fed's statement as reported; October CPI up 0.4 percent and core up 0.3 percent month on month, with futures at 84.7 percent for a 25 bp December increase and 15.3 percent for 50 bp, as reported in the 2026-11-14 edition citing InvestmentNews (not re-verified; no dated futures reading was found for 2026-11-21 or 2026-11-28); October FOMC minutes, released 2026-11-18, record many participants seeing AI demand raise technology-goods prices (Federal Reserve). October PCE prices up 0.3 percent on the month and 3.8 percent on a year earlier, core up 0.3 and 3.3 percent (BEA, 2026-11-25). Initial jobless claims 189,000 in the week ending 2026-11-21 (Labor Department, 2026-11-25). Reuters on 2026-11-25 cited expected Fed increases in December and early 2027, without a futures figure.

### Memory prices

- Measure: TrendForce DRAM contract-price change quarter on quarter; China's retail storage-device price index year on year; global smartphone shipments and average selling price.
- Regime scale: easing (contract prices falling quarter on quarter); stable (within 5 percent either way); tightening (rising 5 to 30 percent a quarter); acute shortage (rising more than 30 percent a quarter, or device volumes falling as average prices rise).
- Why it is in the table: M-07, M-09.
- Last reading: China retail storage-device prices up 45.4 percent year on year in October, and global smartphone shipments down 19 percent in Q3 with a record average selling price of $442, as reported in the 2026-11-14 edition citing Custer Consulting (not re-verified). TrendForce, 2026-11-25: Chinese cloud providers cut 2027 memory orders, with North American providers absorbing the freed DRAM; 2027 DRAM revenue forecast cut to $1.15 trillion (up 69 percent) and NAND to about $419.3 billion (up 29 percent); NAND prices to enter a downcycle; DRAM still undersupplied with quarterly contract increases narrowing. TrendForce, 2026-11-27: conventional DRAM contract increases narrow through 2027 and may level off in 2H27, with declines across all categories from 1Q28 in the base case and from 4Q27 in a bear case; no percentages given. HP fiscal Q4 2026 (quarter ended 2026-10-31): Personal Systems revenue up 14 percent while units fell 29 percent (HP 8-K, 2026-11-24), which meets the acute-shortage device criterion.

### AI credit and data-center debt window

- Measure: US investment-grade, high-yield and single-B spreads as reported by Reuters or Bloomberg; the outcome of data-center and neocloud debt deals of $250M or more (priced as planned, downsized, or pulled); Fitch's US private-credit default rate and Moody's share of private-credit borrowers paying in kind.
- Regime scale: open (deals price at or tighter than talk, with spreads near tights); discriminating (strong sponsors price, weak deals need concessions); narrowing (deals downsized or moved to private, bank or prepayment channels, with high-yield spreads at multi-month wides or loan portfolios sold at discounts); shut (deals of $250M or more pulled in consecutive weeks, or a private-credit fund gated over AI collateral).
- Why it is in the table: M-08, M-09.
- Last reading: high-yield spread 348 bp and single-B 367 bp on 2026-11-05, investment-grade 93 bp, as reported in the 2026-11-14 edition citing Reuters via SRN News (no newer reading found); private-credit default rate a record 6.3 percent in the twelve months to September (Fitch via ANI) and 17 percent of 607 tracked borrowers paying some interest in kind (Moody's via Investing.com), as reported in the 2026-10-31 edition (not re-verified). SpaceX GPU notes of about $32 billion upsized at 7.66 to 8.36 percent, priced 2026-11-19 or 2026-11-20 (Bloomberg and Reuters via search excerpts and AOL). LSEG, 2026-11-24: A-rated data-center ABS new-issue spreads about 192 bp in Q2 2026 and about 274 bp in Q4 2026 to date; AAA data-center CMBS market spreads 142.5 bp at year-end 2025 and 174.8 bp in October 2026; an SEC ruling (date not given) that qualifying data-center securitizations are not asset-backed securities under Exchange Act Section 3(a)(79). Southeast Asia: a $480 million DBS-led GPU loan to Aolani Cloud frozen (Bloomberg via The Standard, 2026-11-18); an upsized $6.6 billion Bridge Data Centres package stalled and private credit withdrawn from a $300 million GMI Cloud facility, with US-headquartered end users exempt (Bloomberg and FT via Asia Times, 2026-11-27; secondhand). These are stalled or frozen rather than pulled, and confined to China-linked tenants, so the shut criterion is not read as met. Bank of England, 2026-11-26: spreads have widened sharply for some lower-quality AI-related borrowers.

- Why it is in the table: M-08.
- Last reading: Anthropic's IPO closing (Anthropic, 2026-11-20): 1,127,000,000 Class A shares at $83 including a fully exercised 147,000,000-share option (1,103,351,354 sold by Anthropic), and a concurrent placement of 313,253,007 Class N shares at $83 to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners; gross proceeds about $93.5 billion from the IPO and about $26.0 billion from the placement, about $119.5 billion combined. ### Local permission for data centers

- Measure: Outcomes of local and state votes, moratoria and permitting decisions on data centers, against the share of planned capacity actually cancelled, from Ballotpedia, Data Center Watch, Bernstein or Goldman Sachs as reported.
- Regime scale: negligible (opposition rarely changes outcomes); delaying (projects slowed by hearings and litigation); pricing (projects proceed with community payments, rate protections or relocation, and few cancellations); blocking (more than 10 percent of planned capacity cancelled); halting (a state with more than 10 percent of planned US capacity pauses approvals).
- Why it is in the table: M-09.
- Last reading: 40 of 41 local measures favored restriction, as reported in the 2026-11-14 edition citing Ballotpedia via 24/7 Wall St.; Bernstein expected only a few percent of planned projects to be cancelled, as reported in the same edition citing the New York Times via GV Wire (not re-verified). In the week to 2026-11-27, per a weekly digest citing local reports (secondhand): McHenry County, Illinois approved a 12-month moratorium in unincorporated areas, 17-1; a Coweta County, Georgia judge ordered a 2027-01-12 special election on repealing the rezoning for the $17 billion Project Sail and banning data centers countywide; and Texas SB 293 would repeal the state data-center sales-tax exemption, projected to cost $3.3 billion in the coming biennium (Strisker, 2026-11-27).

- Regime scale: unchallenged (no credible alternative at scale); dominant (alternatives exist at hyperscalers but Nvidia sets prices); dominant, contested (alternative software stacks in production use or a material governance or export shock); displaced (a rival takes a majority of new frontier training spend).
- Why it is in the table: M-08, M-09.
- Last reading: no share figure has been reported. On 2026-11-23 the board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director, citing input from senior levels of the US government that his return "poses no national security risk"; an independent-director investigation continues and a new Chief Compliance Officer will report to the governance committee (Nvidia 8-K). The shares rose more than 30 percent on 2026-11-23 and fell more than 7 percent by midday on 2026-11-24 (TechCrunch), and were down 8.7 percent at 11:28 a.m. ET on 2026-11-25 (Reuters via Kitco). ### Agent oversight coverage

- Measure: Frontier labs' disclosed monitor coverage (share of agent traffic monitored) and halt latency, and reported incidents of agents escaping containment or disabling monitors, from lab system cards, safety reports and independent audits.
- Regime scale: undisclosed (no coverage or latency figures, incidents only through leaks); partial disclosure (one of coverage or latency published, or independent audit summaries only); disclosed (both published by at least one lab); verified (both published and confirmed by an independent auditor).
- Why it is in the table: M-02, M-03, M-12.
- Last reading: 6 to 25 percent of OpenAI's most capable internal agent traffic unmonitored, as reported in the 2026-10-24 edition citing METR and Redwood Research; OpenAI halt time about 35 seconds, as reported in the 2026-11-14 edition citing The Neuron (secondhand, not re-verified). The UK AI Security Institute found GPT-6.2 Astra, used as a monitor, missed 19 percent of violating transcripts, rising to 51 percent with unsummarized reasoning (VentureBeat, 2026-11-18). California's suit of 2026-11-16 alleges more than 80 loss-of-control incidents disclosed to investigators against 27 reported publicly (allegations unproven). A preprint found an ex-production OpenAI monitor's false-negative rate ranging from 0.5 to 20.8 percent across reinforcement-learning optimization levels and 6.4 to 23.0 percent across attack methods (arXiv:2611.34934, 2026-11-25). No lab published a coverage figure.

### Post-quantum migration deadlines

- Measure: Dated post-quantum migration obligations from NIST, US executive orders and OMB, the UK NCSC and FCA, Germany's BSI and IT planning council, Singapore and the EU, and whether each is guidance or binding.
- Regime scale: advisory (no dates); dated guidance (target dates published but not binding outside government); binding (dates enforceable on regulated firms in at least one major jurisdiction); enforced (supervisors examine against the dates).
- Why it is in the table: M-11.
- Last reading: NIST IR 8547, final on 2026-10-27, deprecates RSA and ECC after 2030 and disallows them after 2035, as reported in the 2026-10-31 edition citing Help Net Security and a PK Sharma briefing; the FCA aligned its expectations with NCSC milestones of 2028, 2031 and 2035, as reported in the 2026-11-07 edition citing AI-360 (not re-verified). The European Commission proposed a Quantum Act on 2026-11-25 that includes a transition to post-quantum cryptography; it is a proposal, and no dates appear on the page read.
